Five tools, one book

Each section answers one question a banker asks before a pitch. All of them run on the same records.

  1. 01 Market Capital, round counts and round sizes by sector and stage, with the last six months set against the six before.
  2. 02 Origination Private companies ranked by the chance they announce a round within twelve months, from a model tested on outcomes it never saw.
  3. 03 Investors Describe a client's sector, stage and cheque size, and get the funds most likely to back it, with the deals behind each match.
  4. 04 Buyers Acquirers ranked for a target's sector by relevance, appetite and how recently they have bought.
  5. 05 Valuation A value range from listed peers, a growth-and-margin regression and Indian private rounds, drawn as a football field.
